Product basics
Adistar is an Adidas running range for Customers who want maximum cushioning, reliable comfort, and support for longer distance running. It is a strong option when a Customer runs further, prefers an easy pace, or asks for a soft and stable feel underfoot.
Recommend Adidas Adistar when Customers say they want to:
run longer distances,
feel more cushioning during easy-paced runs,
get support when they run further,
feel stable while running longer distances, or
have reliable grip for regular running.
Key benefits: Repetitor cushioning, Guidance Rail (Stability Frame) support, Lighttraxion grip, and Adiwear durability help Customers feel cushioned, supported, and secure during longer easy-paced runs.

Key selling message
Use a simple flow: ask one question, listen for the Customer’s cushioning, support, and distance need, then recommend one Adidas Adistar option with one clear benefit.
Ask: “What distance are you running, and do you want the shoe to feel more cushioned, stable, or both?”
Listen: Is the Customer talking about longer runs, easy-paced running, maximum cushioning, stability, support, or grip?
Recommend: Match the shoe to the Customer’s need and explain one clear benefit.
Example recommendation: “Because you are running longer distances and want a more stable cushioned feel, I would show you the Adistar Charge. It gives you maximum cushioning with added stability and grip for distance running.”
